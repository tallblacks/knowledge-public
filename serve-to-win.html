  • 小麦中的主要碳水化合物——支链淀粉，比其他碳水化合物更容易被人体分解。虽然其他食物中也含有支链淀粉，但小麦中的这种特定形式的支链淀粉比其他类型的支链淀粉更容易被分解并转化为葡萄糖。简而言之，它就像一列通往葡萄糖的特快列车。 The primary carbohydrate in wheat, amylopectin, is broken down by the body faster and more efficiently than other carbohydrates. While amylopectin occurs in other foods, the specific version that appears in wheat is actually broken down and converted into glucose more easily than even other varieties of amylopectin. In short, it’s like an express train for glucose.
